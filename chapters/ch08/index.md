# 8 Rank and Fundamental Subspaces

Row reduction reveals four subspaces tied to a matrix $A$: column space, row space, null space, and left null space. **Rank** and **rank–nullity** organize their dimensions. **Coordinates** and **change of basis** describe vectors relative to a chosen basis.
